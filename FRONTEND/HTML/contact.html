Common Questions

FAQ

Are the workout plans suitable for beginners?

+

Absolutely! Our Beginner Plan is specifically designed for people new to fitness. It starts with bodyweight exercises and gradually introduces weights, making it perfect for anyone starting their fitness journey.

Do I need a gym membership to follow the plans?

+

The Beginner Plan can be done with minimal equipment at home. The Intermediate and Advanced Plans are optimized for a gym setting but can be adapted with resistance bands and adjustable dumbbells.

How accurate is the calorie calculator?

+

Our calorie calculator uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, which is considered one of the most accurate methods for estimating daily caloric needs. However, individual metabolism varies, so use it as a starting point and adjust based on results.

Can I switch between plans?

+

Yes! We recommend completing at least 4-6 weeks of a plan before switching. If the Beginner Plan feels too easy after a few weeks, move to Intermediate. Listen to your body and progress at your own pace.
