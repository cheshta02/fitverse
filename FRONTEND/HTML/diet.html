The Truth About Supplements

Supplements support a solid diet - they do not replace one. Only a handful of supplements have strong scientific backing. Focus your budget on these proven essentials before considering anything else. Quality matters - look for third-party tested products.

Essential Supplements

Tier 1 - Must Have

Creatine Monohydrate

The most researched supplement in sports science. 5g daily improves strength, power, and muscle gains. No loading phase needed. Take at any time daily.

5g/dayDaily
Tier 1 - Must Have

Whey Protein

Convenient way to hit protein targets. 1-2 scoops daily as needed. Best post-workout or between meals. Choose isolate for lactose sensitivity.

25-50g/dayAs Needed
Tier 1 - Must Have

Vitamin D3

Essential if you get limited sunlight. Supports bone health, immune function, and testosterone levels. Take with a fat-containing meal for absorption.

2000-5000 IUDaily
Tier 2 - Recommended

Omega-3 Fish Oil

Anti-inflammatory, supports heart and brain health. Look for high EPA/DHA content. Essential if you do not eat fatty fish regularly.

1-3g EPA+DHADaily
Tier 2 - Recommended

Magnesium

Supports sleep quality, muscle function, and recovery. Magnesium glycinate or citrate forms are best absorbed. Take before bed.

200-400mgBefore Bed

What to Avoid

Creatine - EvidenceStrong
Whey Protein - EvidenceStrong
BCAAs - EvidenceWeak
Fat Burners - EvidenceVery Weak
Test Boosters - EvidenceVery Weak

Tips

  • BCAAs are unnecessary if you eat enough protein - save your money
  • Fat burners are largely ineffective - caloric deficit is what burns fat
  • Testosterone boosters have virtually no evidence - skip them entirely
  • Always choose third-party tested supplements (NSF, Informed Sport)
  • Start with food first - supplements are the last 5%, not the foundation
